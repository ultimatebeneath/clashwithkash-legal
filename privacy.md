# Privacy Policy: Clash with Kash Bases

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Clash with Kash Bases ("the app") is an unofficial fan app for Clash of Clans players. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

## What stays on your phone

The app stores these on your device only: your saved (favourite) bases, timer reminders you turn on, polls you have voted in, your settings (such as Reduce motion), and cached images and lists so the app works faster and offline. You can remove cached images any time in More → Settings → Clear cache, or by uninstalling the app.

## What the app sends over the internet

- **Content requests.** To show base layouts, updates, giveaways, vouchers and polls, the app reads public content from our database host, Supabase. Like any internet request, this lets the host see your IP address and basic device and network details.
- **Poll votes.** When you vote in a poll, the app sends the poll and the option you chose to our website's voting service so the result can be counted. No account or personal details are attached.
- **Images** are loaded from the addresses stored with each item.

## Notifications

If you turn on a timer reminder, the app asks for notification permission and shows a local reminder on your phone. You can turn this off at any time in your phone's settings.

## Links to other services

Copy Base opens Clash of Clans. Contact, YouTube, Instagram, X, Terms and Privacy links open other apps or websites. Those services have their own privacy policies.

## Ads and analytics

This version of the app has no advertising, no accounts, no payments and no analytics tools. If this ever changes, we will update this policy first.

## Children

The app is a guide and tool for a game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
