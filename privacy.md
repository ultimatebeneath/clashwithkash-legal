# Privacy Policy: Clash with Kash Bases

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Clash with Kash Bases ("the app") is an unofficial fan app for Clash of Clans players. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we ourselves do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. The app shows ads provided by Google, which may collect some device information as described in the "Advertising" section below.

## What stays on your phone

The app stores these on your device only: your saved (favourite) bases, timer reminders you turn on, polls you have voted in, your settings (such as Reduce motion), your ad choice (if you were asked), and cached images and lists so the app works faster and offline. You can remove cached images any time with the gear icon at the top of the app, then Clear cache, or by uninstalling the app.

## What the app sends over the internet

- **Content requests.** To show base layouts, updates, giveaways, vouchers and polls, the app reads public content from our database host, Supabase. Like any internet request, this lets the host see your IP address and basic device and network details.
- **Poll votes.** When you vote in a poll, the app sends the poll and the option you chose to our website's voting service so the result can be counted. No account or personal details are attached.
- **Images** are loaded from the addresses stored with each item.
- **Ads.** To show ads, the app connects to Google's advertising service. See "Advertising" below.

## Advertising

The app is free and is supported by advertising. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. We do not control which ads are shown, and we do not receive your personal information from advertisers.

To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as:

- your device's advertising ID,
- your IP address and an approximate location worked out from it,
- device and app information (for example device model, operating system version and app version),
- how you interact with ads (for example views and taps).

Google may use this information for personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your choices and where you live. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and Google's Privacy Policy at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Your ad choices**

- **Consent.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your choice about ads and data use when you first open it. You can change your choice at any time with the gear icon at the top of the app, under "Ad privacy settings".
- **Android settings.** You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your phone's settings (usually Settings, then Privacy, then Ads; the exact path depends on your phone).

## Notifications

If you turn on a timer reminder, the app asks for notification permission and shows a local reminder on your phone. You can turn this off at any time in your phone's settings.

## Links to other services

Copy Base opens Clash of Clans. Contact, YouTube, Instagram, X, Terms and Privacy links open other apps or websites. If you tap an ad, you may be taken to an advertiser's app, store page or website. Those services have their own privacy policies.

## Accounts, payments and analytics

The app has no accounts and no payments, and nothing is sold inside the app. We do not use analytics tools of our own. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

## Children

The app is a guide and tool for a game. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
